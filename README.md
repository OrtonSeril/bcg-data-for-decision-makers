# BCG Data for Decision Makers – Marketing Analytics Case Study

## Overview
This project presents a marketing analytics case study completed as part of the BCG Data for Decision Makers virtual experience program on The Forage.

The objective was to analyze customer acquisition performance across multiple marketing channels and recommend where to increase investment to maximize new customer sales.

## Business Problem
A company was running two campaigns (Campaign A and Campaign B) across three channels:

- Email
- Instagram
- Web Banner

The goal was to determine which combination generated the highest number of new customers and which channel produced the strongest overall revenue.

## Analysis Performed
- Cleaned and reviewed the provided dataset
- Compared new customer sales by campaign and channel
- Evaluated total revenue by channel
- Identified the most effective acquisition strategy
- Developed a data-driven business recommendation

## Key Insight
Email + Campaign A consistently generated the highest number of new customer sales.

Email also produced the strongest overall revenue performance across all channels.

## Recommendation
Increase investment in Email + Campaign A to maximize customer acquisition and overall marketing effectiveness.
